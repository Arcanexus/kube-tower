To register another cluster, create on it the read-only ServiceAccount from
deploy/remote-cluster-rbac.yaml, then add an entry to the `clusters` value (inline credentials
or existingSecret) or a Secret labelled kube-tower.arcanexus.com/secret-type=cluster
in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.
